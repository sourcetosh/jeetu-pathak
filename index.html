I

The stars found me first

I was fifteen when my father brought home the Bhrigu Samhita. I set out to prove it wrong.

How does one come to know?Ashtavakra Gita 1.1

II

A mind of evidence

I became a scientist, and trusted only what I could see under a microscope.

Accept what stands to reason, even from a child.Yoga Vasishtha 2.18

III

What a life can become

Every stem cell carries a purpose. I didn’t yet know mine.

Waves, foam and bubbles are not other than water.Ashtavakra Gita 2.4

IV

As within, so above

Then Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar initiated me, and I began to look within.

To the light that shines outside and within, I bow.Yoga Vasishtha 1.1

V

The inner laboratory

I left my research to become a full-time seeker. My own life became the experiment.

If you seek freedom, take patience, honesty, kindness, contentment and truth as nectar.Ashtavakra Gita 1.2

VI

Two in the morning

A few pages of the Yoga Vasishtha every night, until surrender came.

“I am not the doer.” Drink this, and be at ease.Ashtavakra Gita 1.8

VII

From stars to stillness

People wear gemstones to change their stars. I found the change begins within.

You are not earth, water, fire, air or space. You are the witness of them all.Ashtavakra Gita 1.3

VIII

The sand already falling

Prarabdha is the karma already in motion. I stopped fighting what was not meant to be.

Hardship and plenty each come in their own time.Ashtavakra Gita 11.3

IX

The fire that never goes out

At Manikarnika, where the fires never go out, death showed me what a life is for.

Burn the forest of not-knowing in the fire of knowing, and be free of sorrow.Ashtavakra Gita 1.9

X

What I was made for

I gave my effort to what I was truly made for, not to doors that were never mine.

Our tendencies flow like a river. With effort, turn it toward the good.Yoga Vasishtha

XI

No planet to fear

A chart is not a verdict. It shows the lessons a soul came to learn in this lifetime.

Think yourself free, and you are free.Ashtavakra Gita 1.11

XII

A lamp for others

Today I sit with people in their own dark seasons, and help them find their light.

Ah! I am untouched, at peace, awareness itself.Ashtavakra Gita 2.1

Here, now

What is your life asking of you?

You don’t have to walk it alone.

This very moment, you can be at peace.Ashtavakra Gita 1.4
